# Data credits

`kanji.json` is built by `tools/karatto/build-kanji.mjs` from:

- JLPT kanji levels: Jonathan Waller (tanos.co.uk), CC BY, via davidluzgouveia/kanji-data
- KANJIDIC2: Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG), CC BY-SA 4.0 (readings, meanings, frequency)
- JMdict: EDRDG, CC BY-SA 4.0 (common words), via @johnmorrisdotca/jisho

`kanji.json` is a derived work and is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
