Per bone, in its parent's space:
position  = bake + (on − muted)
rotation  = bake × (muted⁻¹ × on)
scale     = bake × on / muted
unchanged bone → keeps the bake's own track
Edited bone: change from the bake (°)
Untouched bones: farthest from the bake (cm, true size)
