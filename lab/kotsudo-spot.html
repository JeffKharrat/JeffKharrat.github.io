What Kotsudo does, per control, per frame
  1. Rename the axes: Unreal (X, Y, Z) = Maya (X, Z, Y). Both in cm.
  2. The move since rest, in world space: Δ = posed × rest⁻¹.
  3. Apply Δ to the Unreal control's own rest, about the Maya pivot: the target (pose_harness._posed_control).
  4. set_global_transform(control, target): Unreal removes the parent and the offset itself and keeps its own numbers.
