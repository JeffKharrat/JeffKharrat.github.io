Jeff Kharrat, character rigging and technical animation

Character rigger and technical animator based in Tokyo. I build production rigs in Maya and Unreal Engine, from dozens of props on Miraculous to the main rigs of the festival-selected short AMARI. At Tokyo University of Technology, I research rebuilding Maya rigs automatically as Unreal Control Rigs.
